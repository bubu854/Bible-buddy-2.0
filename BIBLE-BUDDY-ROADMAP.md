# Bible Buddy Feature Roadmap

This document records requested features and groups them into the app's expandable home-screen categories. Items marked **Planned** are design/product plans, not claims that the feature is already working.

## Profile design — current UI update
- **Implemented in the profile UI:** Instagram/TikTok-inspired profile header, profile photo control, follower/following/post counts, story highlights, Posts/Videos/Saved tabs, pinned-post area, favourite Bible verse, faith-badge area, and one gear icon in the top-right corner.
- **Implemented in the profile UI:** the gear opens one consolidated Profile settings panel for theme, username, display name, bio, favourite verse, and public/private visibility.
- **Still planned:** real story publishing, video uploads, pinned-post management, working likes/comments, saved-post syncing, profile music selection, and live follower statistics.

## 01 — Bible
- Keep the Bible reader, translations, audio Bible, connected verses, timeline, and Bible places together.

## 02 — Buddy AI
- **Daily encouragement:** a short, uplifting message from Buddy each day.
- Buddy personality, voice, language, and Bible-study assistance.

## 03 — Bible Study
- **Bible reading streak:** track consecutive days of Bible reading, with gentle grace for missed days.
- Reading plans, deep study, sermon intelligence, journal, and Scripture memorisation.

## 04 — Bible Adventures
- **Faith badges:** earn badges for milestones such as completing a reading plan, memorising verses, or finishing a study.
- **Bible Buddy XP:** earn experience points for Bible reading, quizzes, memory practice, prayer and study; XP should reward consistency without making faith feel like a competition.
- Bible story mode, level paths, quizzes, and group challenges.

## 05 — Prayer and Worship
- **Favourite worship song:** let each member display a favourite worship song on their profile.
- **Quiet time mode:** a calm, distraction-reduced screen for prayer, worship and Bible reading.
- Personal prayer journal, worship playlists, prayer timer, daily reminders and verse of the day.

## 06 — Wallpapers
- Verse Wallpaper Maker and photo/crop tools.

## 07 — Creativity
- Bible Comic Creator, Christian Sticker Maker, Verse Animation Studio, Worship Poster Maker, and Christian Profile Designer.

## 08 — Settings
- App language, appearance, Buddy preferences, notification preferences and account controls.

## 09 — Bible Games
- Levels, challenges, quiz battles, Bible detective, memory games and rewards.

## 10 — Community
- **Christian For You page:** a discovery feed for Christian posts, testimonies, Bible tips and encouraging content.
- **Christian Reels:** short-form Christian videos, with controls to report, block and hide content.
- **Christian friendships:** find and connect with other members through opt-in friend requests and privacy controls.
- **Prayer wall:** share prayer requests and respond with supportive messages such as “I'm praying for you.”
- Community feed, stories, study groups, comments, likes and group challenges.

## Newly added feature starters (2026-11-15)\n- **Bible Fact Checker:** interactive starter set of common claims with Scripture references and context.\n- **Bible World Explorer:** selectable biblical places with short descriptions and references.\n- **Original Language Explorer:** introductory Hebrew and Greek word entries with context notes.\n- **Christian Life Coach:** selectable everyday situations with practical, Bible-based reflection and safety guidance.\n- **Offline Bible Buddy:** saves the app shell and local feature scripts in browser cache; it does not yet bundle full Bible translations for offline reading.\n\n## More ideas to consider

### Profile and identity
- Faith journey timeline with milestones the member chooses to share.
- Profile verse banner that can be changed weekly.
- Testimony section with audience controls.
- “Currently studying” and “Currently praying for” profile badges.
- Profile theme packs for seasons such as Advent, Easter and summer.

### Community
- Encouragement cards that friends can send privately.
- Small-group Bible reading circles with shared progress.
- Church and youth-group event board.
- Weekly community question and Bible polls.
- A moderated testimony library with optional anonymous sharing.
- Comment filters, block/report tools and strong teen-friendly privacy defaults.

### Bible growth
- Bible book completion map.
- Verse memory reminders with spaced repetition.
- “What did I learn today?” one-minute reflection.
- Personal Bible goals with flexible rest days.
- Audio verse of the day.
- Bible topic pathways such as hope, courage, forgiveness and prayer.

### Prayer and worship
- Gratitude moments: save one thing to thank God for each day.
- Answered-prayer journal with private/public choice.
- Worship setlist builder for personal or group use.
- Gentle quiet-time timer with optional ambient sounds.
- Prayer partner matching only by mutual opt-in, with privacy and safety controls.

### Buddy AI
- “Explain it simply” and “Go deeper” answer modes.
- Weekly Bible study recap.
- Turn a study into flashcards or a quiz.
- Help create a personal reading plan based on available time.
- Encouragement messages that can be switched off or scheduled.

## Product principles
- Keep the app's black and navy-blue visual identity with restrained gold accents.
- Make English the default and preserve full Faroese support.
- Keep profile controls behind one gear icon.
- Ask before sharing prayer requests, testimony, profile details or activity publicly.
- Do not present planned features as live until their screens, data storage, permissions and safety controls are implemented and tested.
