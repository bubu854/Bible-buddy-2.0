THE STORY OF SCRIPTURE

Bible timeline.

A broad overview of major biblical events. Dates are approximate and debated.

  1. Creation and the early world

    Genesis 1–11 · Creation, the Fall, the Flood and Babel.

  2. Patriarchs

    Genesis 12–50 · Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph.

  3. Exodus and the wilderness

    Exodus–Deuteronomy · Moses, the Exodus and the covenant at Sinai.

  4. Israel in the land

    Joshua–Ruth · Settlement, judges and Ruth's story.

  5. Kings and the divided kingdom

    1 Samuel–2 Chronicles · Saul, David, Solomon and the divided kingdoms.

  6. Exile and return

    2 Kings, Daniel, Ezra and Nehemiah · Exile in Babylon and return.

  7. Life and ministry of Jesus

    Matthew–John · Birth, teaching, death and resurrection of Jesus.

  8. The early Church

    Acts and the letters · The Gospel spreads across the Roman world.

  9. Revelation

    Revelation · John's visions and the Christian hope of God's renewal.
